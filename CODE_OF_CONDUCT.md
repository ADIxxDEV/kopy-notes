# Code of Conduct

Kopy Notes welcomes teachers, students, developers, designers and first-time contributors. Everyone deserves a respectful environment, regardless of background, identity, age, disability, experience or technical ability.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience and respect. Critique ideas and code, rather than the person.
- Ask questions constructively, explain decisions and make room for different experiences.
- Help keep discussions accessible and focused on the project.
- Respect privacy, consent and the licenses of contributed work.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them and accept constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, personal attacks, targeted humiliation and deliberate intimidation are not welcome. Neither are doxxing, publishing private messages or personal information without permission, spam, impersonation, or repeated disruption after a maintainer asks it to stop. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Where this applies

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other community spaces managed by Kopy Notes. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly. It does not prohibit respectful disagreement or good-faith criticism.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the project maintainer, [@ADIxxDEV](https://github.com/ADIxxDEV), privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open an issue titled **Private conduct reporting channel requested**, without identifying anyone or describing sensitive details, and ask the maintainer for a private channel.

Include relevant links, dates and context when a private channel is established. Do not post personal information or allegations in public issues. For a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will handle reports with reasonable care for privacy, share details only when needed to address the concern, and avoid involving a maintainer who is personally involved where an independent reviewer is available. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. If someone is in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, limit participation, close disruptive discussions or restrict repository access. Responses depend on the severity, context and any repeated behavior:

1. A request to correct the behavior, with an explanation.
2. A warning and, where appropriate, a pause in participation.
3. A temporary restriction for serious or repeated misconduct.
4. A permanent ban for severe harassment, threats, retaliation or continued misconduct.

Serious incidents may require an immediate restriction. Maintainers should explain decisions privately where practical and give the affected person a chance to provide relevant context. A person may request reconsideration through the same private reporting channel; an uninvolved maintainer should review it when available.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are accountable to this policy too. Enforce it consistently and proportionately, respect good-faith reports, and explain participation expectations clearly. This policy guides community moderation; it does not promise a staffed support service or replace applicable law.
